Stay focused. Know where your time goes.

Promoscope is a Pomodoro timer built into your browser that automatically tracks which websites you visit during each work session. When the timer ends, you get a clear visual breakdown of your time — no manual logging required.

How it works

1. Start a Pomodoro session from the extension popup
2. Work as you normally do — Promoscope quietly tracks your active tab in the background
3. When the session ends, see a chart showing exactly how your time was distributed across websites

Features

● Pomodoro timer with work, break, and long break cycles
● Automatic website tracking by domain — no setup, no manual input
● Visual pie chart showing time distribution after each session
● Daily stats: completed rounds and total work time at a glance
● Badge countdown on the extension icon so you always know how much time is left
● Notification alert when a session ends
● Customizable durations for work, break, and long break periods
● Supports English and Traditional Chinese

Why install Promoscope?

Most Pomodoro apps only give you a timer. Promoscope goes further — it answers the question: "Where did my time actually go?" Without lifting a finger, you get an honest picture of your focus habits each session.

Whether you're a developer, student, writer, or remote worker, Promoscope helps you stay accountable to yourself — not through guilt, but through awareness.

Privacy first

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking beyond your own browser.
